# Board brief: Immo-Cashflow

## The idea (from founder/idea.md)

- What it is: Eine App/Web-Plattform, mit der private Immobilieninvestoren in Österreich prüfen, ob sich ein Objekt rentiert und ob ihr monatlicher Cashflow nach der Kreditrate positiv oder negativ ist, und ihr Immobilienportfolio tracken (Wert, Tilgung, Restschuld).
- Who it is for: Erstinvestoren in Österreich, die gerade ihre erste Vorsorge- bzw. Anlagewohnung suchen oder gekauft haben.
- What it sells, at what price: Zugang zur Plattform für 19 € pro Jahr. Ob es daneben eine Gratisversion gibt, ist noch offen.
- Where and how: Online, ganz Österreich. Vertrieb und Kanäle noch offen.
- Budget and constraints: Noch offen (Startbudget, Zeit, Team, Entwicklungskosten nicht genannt).

## History the founder shared

An earlier version of the idea was pitched to a simulated buyer panel (20 language-model buyers, not real customers; buy rates from such panels are an upper bound). That version: free download with unlimited deal analyses but some metrics hidden, 29 € one-off PDF export, or 7,99 € per month for everything, aimed at all private investors in Austria (first-timers, small landlords with 1-3 flats, investors with 4-10 properties).

Result: 1 of 20 would pay (one month, then likely cancel). Nearly all would download the free version.
- Reasons for passing: own Excel spreadsheet already does it (8), no need before owning a property / only buy every few years (5), do not trust an unknown app with loan data (3), want everything in Parqet instead of a second app (2), price (1).
- First-time buyers: 0 of 9 would pay; portfolio tracking is useless to them until they own something.
- Recurring doubt: are the Austrian specifics right (Grunderwerbsteuer, Eintragungsgebühr, Richtwertmiete, AfA, Rücklage)?
- What would flip a no: proof the numbers are correct for Austria (ideally checked by a tax adviser), no manual typing (Excel/bank import, Parqet sync), a visible Austrian team with Impressum and EU data storage, interest-rate scenarios per property, a yearly or one-off price instead of a monthly subscription.
- Most panel buyers called the monthly plan expensive from about 6 € and too expensive from about 10 €.

The new pitch (19 € per year, focused on first-time investors) is the founder's response. It has not been tested yet.

## Not known yet
Market size, number of first-time investors per year in Austria, customer acquisition cost, development and running costs, competitors, the founder's budget and skills.
